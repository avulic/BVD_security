Who We Are

BVD Security d.o.o.

BVD Security d.o.o. is a licensed security company operating in accordance with the Private Protection Act of the Republic of Croatia and all applicable regulations of the Ministry of the Interior.

The company was founded with the clear goal of providing highly professional, lawful, and reliable private protection services to persons, assets, and events. Our work is based on precise risk assessment, preventive action, and clearly defined security procedures.

BVD Security operates exclusively through professionally trained, licensed, and vetted personnel, with complete discretion, responsibility, and professional integrity.
